# Dossier Management
## General
Larx is a secure Software as a Service (SaaS) platform designed to streamline the relationship between your law firm and your clients. Far more than just a branded client portal, Larx serves as your central document management system and a dynamic environment where lawyers and clients collaborate seamlessly. It acts as a secure, trusted pillar supporting your firm's digital presence.
##### Client PortalYour 
clients gain access to a personalized, secure, and controlled online dashboard where they can view their assigned dossiers. Files can be uploaded to a dossier at any time, from any location, by any authorized user, ensuring smooth and immediate information sharing.
##### Document Management System
Dossiers can be managed completely from a single, centralized interface. Firm administrators can grant or revoke access rights to any dossier on the fly, create or remove dossiers, and manage client profiles. Whenever a dossier is updated, all authorized participants automatically receive an email notification to keep everyone aligned.
##### Collaboration Environment
Dossiers in Larx are more than just static data repositories; they are active communication channels. Authorized participants can securely discuss case details, facts, and updates directly within the built-in dossier chat module. By adding immediate context and collaborating in real time, teams can work efficiently toward the best possible legal outcome. The dossier becomes the single source of truth for both your firm and your clients, where files and context live under one roof.
##### Centralized Communication
Larx allows your firm to gather and organize valuable information about your clients' profiles. Based on these collected insights, you can manage targeted email communications. This enables you to reach specific client segments with personalized updates based on their industry sector or legal interests, helping you maintain strong client relationships and increase long-term retention.
##### Secure and Trusted Environment
Your firm's client environment is entirely controlled by you. You dictate exactly who has access to which dossier, and permissions can be modified instantly at any time. Furthermore, all dossier updates are centrally logged, providing a clear audit trail so you always know who modified a file or adjusted a setting.
## Setting up your client environment
Below you will find context and instructions how to set up a client in your Larx application and manage the relationships of a client with both dossiers and representatives.
## Create new dossier
A new dossier can be generated from scratch within your master dashboard to house a new legal matter. Larx provides a guided setup procedure to safely initialize the case workspace. A creation wizard will prompt you for fundamental structural metrics, linking the case to a specific managing law firm and client profile from the outset to guarantee strict data isolation.
### Creation process (Wizard)
##### Step 1: general dossier details
In this opening step, you establish the baseline identity of the legal file. You are required to input the following parameters:
- Dossier Name (a clear, recognizable case reference or reference code)
- Matter Description & Legal Purpose (an optional field to add a summary overview of the case context)
##### Step 2: Associate Managing Law Firm
Select the specific registered law firm that holds directory jurisdiction and administrative mandate over this case. This dropdown strictly displays firms already pre-vetted within your system registry.
##### Step 3: Select Client Profile
Map the dossier directly to the target corporate or individual client file. Enforcing this relationship ensure that only representatives linked to this client profile can ever be granted access further down the line.
##### Step 4: Assign Dossier Lead & Legal Personnel
Designate the primary attorney who will serve as the Dossier Lead (the main contact person for the case lifecycle). You can also use this step to select additional lawyers and client representatives from the checked list to immediately grant them folder access.
##### Step 5: Review and Confirm
The wizard concludes with an exhaustive summary displaying your staged case metrics, assigned legal teams, and client access points. Review the parameters for data alignment. If an error is spotted, navigate back to the relevant step to update the state. Once validated, click the final confirmation trigger to provision the dossier workspace and push it live.

## Amend Dossier
Once a dossier is active, you can adapt its data matrices or update its categorizations as the case evolves over time. Navigate to the specific dossier dashboard view and click the edit details button. This launches the dossier modification form, allowing you to alter the following entries:
- Dossier Reference Name
- Matter Description & Legal Purpose
- Required Legal Expertise & Service Classifications


## Delete Dossier
If a dossier record was created in error or must be permanently purged, you can initiate a deletion request. Click the edit trigger in the dossier workspace and select the destructive Delete Action. After confirming the security warning layout, the system executes the following lifecycle changes:
- The primary dossier entry and its unique file directory tree are permanently deleted.
- All active file tracking tables, document indexes, and relational links are cleanly dropped.
- Access Cleanup Security Trigger: All historical lawyer and representative visibility connections mapping to this specific folder are instantly severed.
- Note: This action is irreversible and completely removes the workspace signature from your active folders database.
## Add Lawyer
To scale up your legal defense or add a specialist to a case, you can grant access to secondary attorneys. Click the "Add Lawyer" button within the active dossier panel. A selection modal opens, rendering a list of attorneys tied to the managing law firm who do not yet hold visibility over this file. Check the profile names you wish to authorize and confirm. They will receive an automatic email trigger and gain immediate workspace permissions—allowing them to upload documents, review file history, and post inside the secure case chat.
## Remove Lawyer
If an attorney concludes their work on a case or moves to a different practice group, you can remove them instantly. Select the lawyer’s profile from the active personnel panel within the dossier view and click remove.Access Revocation Boundary: Confirming this action cuts their connection immediately. The lawyer instantly loses all folder visibility, folder update tracking, document management rights, and access to the internal dossier chat timeline. It leaves historical comments intact but bars the user from re-entering the workspace.
## Add Representative
To allow your client to monitor updates, upload requested paperwork, or message the legal team, you can add client-side users to the dossier ecosystem. Click the "Add Representative" button inside the dossier view. A modal will open displaying authorized personnel linked to that specific client profile who are not yet part of the folder. Select the desired contacts and confirm the assignment. They gain immediate file reading and upload privileges and can access the integrated case chat system under your controlled boundary.
## Remove Representative
To revoke a client-side contact’s access to a specific legal matter, utilize the representative removal command within the dossier portal. Select the representative you wish to detach and launch the confirmation screen.Security isolation cascade: Confirming the removal instantly terminates their digital visibility and access credentials for this specific folder pathway. They can no longer look at case summaries, open indexed documents, or read chat updates. However, because this is managed strictly on a per-dossier basis, removing them from this case folder will not affect their active access configurations on any other dossiers belonging to that client.
