# Firm Management
## General
Larx is a secure Software as a Service (SaaS) platform designed to streamline the relationship between your law firm and your clients. Far more than just a branded client portal, Larx serves as your central document management system and a dynamic environment where lawyers and clients collaborate seamlessly. It acts as a secure, trusted pillar supporting your firm's digital presence.
##### Client PortalYour 
clients gain access to a personalized, secure, and controlled online dashboard where they can view their assigned dossiers. Files can be uploaded to a dossier at any time, from any location, by any authorized user, ensuring smooth and immediate information sharing.
##### Document Management System
Dossiers can be managed completely from a single, centralized interface. Firm administrators can grant or revoke access rights to any dossier on the fly, create or remove dossiers, and manage client profiles. Whenever a dossier is updated, all authorized participants automatically receive an email notification to keep everyone aligned.
##### Collaboration Environment
Dossiers in Larx are more than just static data repositories; they are active communication channels. Authorized participants can securely discuss case details, facts, and updates directly within the built-in dossier chat module. By adding immediate context and collaborating in real time, teams can work efficiently toward the best possible legal outcome. The dossier becomes the single source of truth for both your firm and your clients, where files and context live under one roof.
##### Centralized Communication
Larx allows your firm to gather and organize valuable information about your clients' profiles. Based on these collected insights, you can manage targeted email communications. This enables you to reach specific client segments with personalized updates based on their industry sector or legal interests, helping you maintain strong client relationships and increase long-term retention.
##### Secure and Trusted Environment
Your firm's client environment is entirely controlled by you. You dictate exactly who has access to which dossier, and permissions can be modified instantly at any time. Furthermore, all dossier updates are centrally logged, providing a clear audit trail so you always know who modified a file or adjusted a setting.
## Setting up your firm environment
Below you will find context and instructions how to set up a firm in your Larx application and manage the relationships of a firm with dossiers and lawyers.
## Create new firm
A new law firm can be registered in your system within seconds. Larx provides a guided setup procedure to safely initialize the firm profile. A creation wizard will walk you through a series of steps to input core firm metrics. Some details are required to configure the database properly, while others are optional. If you need to onboard a firm quickly, you can complete the mandatory fields and add secondary profile context later using the Amend Firm Details panel.
## Creation process (Wizard)
##### Step 1: general firm details
This initialization step contains the mandatory identification criteria for the law firm profile. You are required to input the following information:
- Official firm name (the registered corporate or legal entity name)
- Primary office address (the central operational headquarters location)
- Main contact 
- Email address (the primary inbox for administrative or billing correspondence)
You can also optinally add the firm's website and core telephone lines. This information is saved to the central registry and can be edited at any time.
##### Step 2: Practice Areas and Jurisdictions
In this step, you can select the specialized legal domains your firm covers, such as Corporate Law, Real Estate, or Tax practice tracks. This helps categorize your firm profiles internally so you can map appropriate dossier frameworks to the correct legal experts later on.
##### Step 3: Review and Confirm
A final summary layout will display all the entered information for verification. Please review the details carefully. If you notice an error, you can jump back to the corresponding step to correct it. Once everything looks accurate, click the confirm button to add the law firm to the database and display it on your master dashboard.
## Amend firm Details
Once a law firm is established, you can update its details or enrich its profile whenever corporate information changes. Navigate to the firm profile view and click the edit button in the upper right corner of your screen. This will open the firm modification page where you can update the following entries:
- Corporate / Legal Entity Name
- Primary Address Details
- Main Correspondence Email
- Active Practice Areas & Classifications
## Add lawyer
You can onboard and assign lawyers to a firm directory to give them operational credentials within the platform. Click the "Add Lawyer" action button from within the firm profile view or the primary dashboard control banner. A setup modal will open prompting you for the following profile details:
- Full name
- Professional email address
- Legal title / Role designation
System Background Trigger: Once you confirm the entry, the application automatically dispatches an onboarding email containing a secure activation token to the new lawyer. Clicking the link takes them to a validation page where they can create their private password, giving them immediate access to the application and their assigned firm dashboard.
## Remove lawyer
If an attorney changes firms or leaves your network, you can remove them directly from the firm directory view. Click the remove button next to the lawyer’s profile to open the validation modal. Review the proposed change to ensure you have selected the correct individual.
Access Revocation Cascade: Confirming this action automatically executes an administrative cleanup. Removing a lawyer from a firm immediately revokes their access rights across every underlying dossier managed by that specific firm. They will instantly lose folder visibility, document management rights, and access to secure case chat channels.
## Create new Client
Firms can initialize new client profiles directly from within the firm management environment. Launching this wizard from the firm profile view automatically maps the background law firm relationship, saving you from entering redundant data fields. You will be guided through the standard client onboarding process, where you can establish the client's official name, assign a client administrator, and configure their corporate sector interests. Once confirmed, the client profile is written to the database and automatically linked to your firm directory.
## Create new Dossier
You can spin up a new case folder for any client associated with your firm directly from the firm dashboard. Click the "Create New Dossier" button to open the setup modal. You will be required to input a clear Dossier Name and an optional matter description. Because you are launching this from the firm workspace, the system automatically tags your law firm as the managing organization. You can then immediately assign a dossier lead, add secondary attorneys, and select client representatives to form a secure workspace under one roof.
## Attach Dossier
If an existing legal dossier in the system needs to be transferred or mapped to your law firm for active management, you can use the Attach Dossier action module. Click the button to launch a selection modal displaying available case folders. Select the target dossier from the list, review the relational alignment signature on the summary step, and click confirm. This action immediately establishes a management relationship, enabling your assigned lawyers to access the folder, review historical documentation, and join active chat channels.
## Detach Dossier
To sever a firm management relationship from a case folder without deleting the underlying historical data, use the Detach Dossier command module. Select the target dossier from your active list and trigger the detachment modal. Review the warning prompt carefully.
Preservation Guard: Confirming the detachment breaks the operational connection between your law firm and the dossier. Your attorneys will immediately lose case visibility and folder permissions. However, the raw dossier files, historical chat contexts, and client metadata are fully preserved in the database registry so they can be securely archived or re-mapped to another firm if necessary.
