# Client Management
## General
Larx is a secure Software as a Service (SaaS) platform designed to streamline the relationship between your law firm and your clients. Far more than just a branded client portal, Larx serves as your central document management system and a dynamic environment where lawyers and clients collaborate seamlessly. It acts as a secure, trusted pillar supporting your firm's digital presence.
##### Your Client Portal 
clients gain access to a personalized, secure, and controlled online dashboard where they can view their assigned dossiers. Files can be uploaded to a dossier at any time, from any location, by any authorized user, ensuring smooth and immediate information sharing.
##### Document Management System
Dossiers can be managed completely from a single, centralized interface. Firm administrators can grant or revoke access rights to any dossier on the fly, create or remove dossiers, and manage client profiles. Whenever a dossier is updated, all authorized participants automatically receive an email notification to keep everyone aligned.
##### Collaboration Environment
Dossiers in Larx are more than just static data repositories; they are active communication channels. Authorized participants can securely discuss case details, facts, and updates directly within the built-in dossier chat module. By adding immediate context and collaborating in real time, teams can work efficiently toward the best possible legal outcome. The dossier becomes the single source of truth for both your firm and your clients, where files and context live under one roof.
##### Marketing Management Engine
Larx allows your firm to gather and organize valuable information about your clients' profiles. Based on these collected insights, you can manage targeted email communications. This enables you to reach specific client segments with personalized updates based on their industry sector or legal interests, helping you maintain strong client relationships and increase long-term retention.
##### Secure and Trusted Environment
Your firm's client environment is entirely controlled by you. You dictate exactly who has access to which dossier, and permissions can be modified instantly at any time. Furthermore, all dossier updates are centrally logged, providing a clear audit trail so you always know who modified a file or adjusted a setting.
## Setting up your client environment
Below you will find context and instructions how to set up a client in your Larx application and manage the relationships of a client with both dossiers and representatives.
## Create new client
A new client can be created in your system within seconds. Larx provides a guided setup procedure for creating a client. A wizard will ask you for certain details in an array of steps. Some details are required, others optional. If you want to create a new client quickly, you can limit yourselves to the required details. In the Amend Client section (hereunder) you can add additional information whenever convenient. 
### Creation process (Wizard)
#### Step 1: general client details
This is the only place in which certain details are mandatory. The following infomation is required:
- Official name (in case of a company: the full legal name)
- Nickname (a short identifiable name)
- Primary correspondence Email address.

You can also add the industry of the client. This information may be relevant for your firm if you want to send targeted emails to clients based on industry characteristics. For instance you may want to inform all your real estate clients per email if certain legal changes in this field are relevant for them. Note that all information in the following steps are optional. If your goal is to create a client quickly, you may ignore these steps and move right to the end of the wizard.
#### Step 2: Legal Expertise Qualifications
If your client requires assistane in certain identifiable legal fields, you can add these in step 2. Note that this information may be used for targeting purposes. For instane you may want to inform all your clients with a VAT interest when your firm adds a new VAT partner to its team. 
#### Step 3 Service and Business needs
In this step you can specific business needs of the client. For instance, if your client needs assistance in the field of VAT, you may want to specifiy this with a need for compliance assistance. 
#### Step 4 Assign Client Admin
In this step you may want to add a client admin. The client admin is the main contact person of the client. This is the person who you want to give access to the firm-client dossier. In this dossier you will find files such as the agreements as concluded between the client and the firm, invoices, client - firm communication (chats) and other material relevant for the relationship between client and firm. Note that in this step you can only select a person who is already in your database. If the required Client Admin is not in the database, you can add a new person in the next step and assign that person the status of Client Admin. Note that the Client Admin has only access to the firm-client dossier. However, you can also assign this person to any dossier (of that client) if so required. 
#### Step 5 Create Client Admin
In this step you can create a new person. This person will be assigned the role of client admin for this specific client. If this person is assigned to other client dossiers, this person will act as a normal Representative of the client in these dossiers. This step requires you to add the following information:
- Full name
- Email address.
You can optinally add the clients avatar (image of the person) if desired. 

Note that the person you have just created will receive an onboardings email, in which the person is requested to click a link. This link will navigate the new client admin to a onboardingspage, in which the client admin is requested to create its own secret password. Adter this, the Client Admin has access to the application, to the firm-client dossier, and any other dossier specifically assigned to this person. 
#### Step 6 Attach to Law Firm
In this step you can add the client to a law firm. Note that you can only select a law firm that is already in the database. If you want to attach the client to a new Law Firm, you have to first create the new law firm, and redo the create new client procedure after the firm is added. Note that you cannot add a lawyer in this step. Lawyers are assigned on a per dossier basis. 
#### Step 7 Add Additional Representative
In this step you can select persons you want to add as a Representative. Note that you can only select a person who is already registered in the application. 
#### Step 8 Review and Confirm
A confirmation screen will be shown, in which all inserted information is visible. Please check the information, if something is wrong, you can go back to the step where the wrong information is inserted, and redo the procedure from there. Once all the information is correct, you can click the confirm button, and the new client is added to your database and visible in the clients dashboard. 
## Amend client
Once a client is created, you can always add new data, or change existing data, of that client. Go to the dossier view, and click the edit button in the upper right corner of your screen. You will be navigated to a edit screen, in which you find the edit and delete button. When clicking the edit button, you will be presented with a "modify client page": You can change the following information:
- Corporate / Legal Entity Name
- Client Nickname (Display Alias)
- Industry
- Email Address*
- Client Interests & Classifications
## Delete client
A client can be deleted. Go to the dossier view, and click the edit button in the upper right corner of your screen. You will be navigated to a edit screen, in which you find the edit and delete button. When clicking the delete button, you will be asked to confirm the deletion, and once this has been done, the following will happen:
- the client will be removed from the database
- all client representatives and the client admin will be removed from the client
- those removed from the client will be removed from all dossiers of the client
- the dossiers won't be deleted, they will remain in the database, but without a relationship with the client. The firm and the assigned lawyers remain having access to the dossier. 
## Add Representative to a client
You can add any person in the database withn the role of a representative as a representative to any client. You can do this from two places:
- from within the client view (click the attach representative button)
- from the sidebar (click the attach representative button)
When clicking from within the client view, a modal will be opened, in which you will see the client to which you want to add the representative. Next to the client you can select the representative you want to add. Once the representative has been selected, you will see a review screen. You can verify it the correct person will be added to the correct client. If this is the case, you click the confirm button, and the new representative is added to the client. Note that this action does not grant access to any client dossier. If you want to add the new representative to any dossier, you have to do that on a per dossier basis. see <link>
## Remove Representative from a client
You can remove a representative from a client from within the client view. Click the remove button, and a modal will be opened. In the modal you will see all representatives which are currently attached to the client. On the right side of the screen you can select the representative you want to remove. Once done you will see a review screen. Please check if the proposed removal is correct. If not you can go back and select another representative to be removed. Once the review screen is correct, you can click confirm, and the representative will be removed from the client. Note that this action will have the consequence that the representative, which is removed from the client, will no longer have access to any dossier of that client. So in case the representative was attached to 3 dossiers of that client, the removed representative will no longer have access to all 3 dossiers. 
## Create new Dossier
#### Step 1: general dossier details
From within the Client View, you can click the Create new Dossier button, and then a modal will be opened. For creating a new dossier, you are required to create a new Dossier name. You can optionally fill in the Matter Description & Legal Purpose field, but it is not required. 
#### Step 2: add dossier to law firm
In this step you can select the law firm which should handle the dossier. If the law firm you want to assign is not in the list, you have to create a new law firm first (via <link>) and then start over again
#### Step 3: add dossier lead
You can select a lawyer which is associated with the selected firm and assign this person as a dossier lead. This person is the primary contact of the dossier. If the person you want to assign as dossier lead is not in the list, you can add a new lawyer to the database in the next step
#### Step 4: add new dossier lead
In this step you can create a new person. This person will be assigned the role of dossier lead. This person is the primary contact of the dossier. This step requires you to add the following information:
- Full name
- Email address.
You can optinally add the clients avatar (image of the person) if desired. 

Note that the person you have just created will receive an onboardings email, in which the person is requested to click a link. This link will navigate the new dossier lead to a onboardingspage, in which the client admin is requested to create its own secret password. Adter this, the dossier lead has access to the application, and to the dossier you are now creating. 
#### Step 5: add additional lawyers
You can select lawyers who are associated with the law firm you have selected under step 2. The selected lawyers will have access to the dossier, may add documents and files to the dossier, and may communicate to other lawyers and representatives of the dossier via the chat functionality. 
#### Step 6: add additional representatives
As you are creating this dossier from within the client view, you can select one or more representatives from the selected client. The selected representatives will have access to the dossier, may add documents and files to the dossier, and may communicate to other lawyers and representatives of the dossier via the chat functionality. 
#### Step 7: Review and Confirm
You will be shown a review screen, in which you see how the new dossier will look like. If you want to change some details, please go back to the step in which you can create the required changes. Once the review sceen is correct, you can click the confirm button and the new dossier is created. You will find the dossier in the Dossier view.
